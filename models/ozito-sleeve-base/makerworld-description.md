# Copy-sleeve base for the Ozito PXC trim router

This base lets you use copy sleeves on the Ozito PXC 18V brushless trim router, model PXBLTS-018.
The equivalent Einhell router is the Einhell TP-ET 18 Li BL.
The base has been tested with the Ozito PXC router.
The base has not been tested with the Einhell router.
The base replaces the router's original base plate.

A copy sleeve is a short tube around the router bit.
The sleeve runs along the edge of a template, so the router cuts the same shape as the template.

The base takes the sleeves and nut from [Copy sleeve Parkside 10-12-14-16-18-20-24-30mm](https://makerworld.com/en/models/191715-copy-sleeve-parkside-10-12-14-16-18-20-24-30mm) by [David Holecko](https://makerworld.com/en/@david.holecko).
This model includes the nut, a 13 mm sleeve, and a 17 mm sleeve. Print other sleeve sizes from the original model.

## Use with IKEA Skadis pegboard templates

The copy sleeves in this model are for router templates that cut IKEA Skadis pegboards:

- the [Skadis Router Template](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1926381-skadis-router-template) by [GKF-Fräser](https://makerworld.com/en/@user_557697631), with the 13 mm copy sleeve
- the [SKADIS template for router](https://makerworld.com/en/models/3227339-skadis-template-for-router) by [Bab-lou_Factory](https://makerworld.com/en/@user_3197404491), with the 17 mm copy sleeve

## How this model remixes the original

The original model has a base for the Parkside trim router.
This model replaces that base with a new base for the Ozito, and adds two sleeve sizes:

- **Base:** the hole and recess for the sleeve are the same as the original, so the original sleeves and nut fit. The screw holes, thickness, and shape are new, to fit the Ozito.
- **13 mm sleeve:** made from the original 12 mm sleeve, with the guide tube widened to 13 mm and its hole widened to 10.5 mm.
- **17 mm sleeve:** made from the original 16 mm sleeve, with the guide tube widened to 17 mm and its hole widened to 14.5 mm.
- **Nut:** the original nut, unchanged.

## What you need

- The 4 screws from the router's original base plate. These screws are M4 countersunk screws, 8 mm long.
- The nut and a sleeve from this model.
- For other sleeve sizes, the sleeves from David Holecko's model.

## Print the base

The base has two sides:

- The **router side** has a large round pocket for the nut, with two small notches at its edge.
- The **workpiece side** is the flat side that touches the wood while you cut. Each screw hole has a wide recess for the screw head.

The files load with the router side down. Print the base this way, without supports.
Do not use the slicer's automatic orientation, because the slicer can turn the base over.

The top of the nut pocket is a ring 4 mm wide that prints over air.
The underside of the ring can droop slightly. A little roughness there does not affect the fit.

## Fit and use the base

- Fit the base so the small notch on its edge points towards the router's dust port.
- With the battery out, turn the bit by hand to check that it does not touch the sleeve.
- The bit reaches 4.65 mm less far below this base than below the original, thinner plate.
